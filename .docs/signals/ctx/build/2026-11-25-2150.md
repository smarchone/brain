# Session 2026-11-25-2150: insights and backlog views in the app
> The app's Insights view becomes a topic map with search and point-list topic pages; a Backlog tab shows dumps; convos inherit their dumps' tags.

## Steer
- Insights view has two levels. Home (`#insights`): a search box over every insight, then a grid of topic cards in index order, each with the topic title, its stance from index.md, its insight count and last update. Search filters insights across topics, grouped by topic, matches highlighted. Topic page (`#insights-<topic>`): title, stance as the lead line, the insights, previous/next topic links and a link back to all topics.
  why: one long scroll of every insight left the user lost; the map shows the whole brain at a glance and a topic page is read alone
  not: one page listing every topic and insight (no overview; every entry weighs the same)
  by: agent
- On a topic page, insights are plain points, one sentence each: no cards, borders, numbers or meta rows. Each ends in one faint source link (→ a convo in the app, ↗ a golden session) that brightens on hover; its date and source name are in the link's tooltip. The `learning:`/`aha:` prefix is not shown.
  why: the user wants to make sense of the topic, not each entry; links must not distract
  not: each insight as a large entry with its date and full source slug under it
  by: human
- Aha insights are shown in the accent blue, bullet included; learnings in the normal text color.
  why: an aha is the connection that clicked, worth spotting at a glance
  not: marking aha with a label or the bullet alone
  by: human
- The app has a Backlog tab: each dump as its title, kind, added date and tags, newest first; its ingest claim shows as a tooltip on hover; clicking opens the claim, gist, key quotes, found-while and a source link. No counts or unread badges. serve.py embeds brain/backlog/ alongside convo/ and insights/.
  why: the user wants to see what is waiting; kept clean with no pile metrics
  not: the claim shown under every title (cluttered); counts or badges; the backlog never shown (why: a visible pile makes people avoid the tool; the pile metrics, not the list, are what's dropped)
  by: human
- A convo's frontmatter has `tags:`: the tags of its dumps, merged in order, duplicates dropped; none when it used no dumps. The app shows a convo's labels then its tags, each filterable; catalog.md lists tags beside labels.
  why: convos had only topic labels while their sources carried descriptive tags; inheriting them makes convos findable by subject
  not: the agent choosing new tags at close; tags only on dumps
  by: human
