# Session 2026-11-25-1950: session opens with a briefing
> Sessions open with a three-line briefing (about, why it matters, walk away with), then the question.

## Steer
- A session opens with a three-line briefing before the first question: what it's about (the cluster and what it pushes on from past insights), why it matters (what changes in the user's work or thinking if it lands), and what they could walk away with (a decision, a revised insight, a sharper question). Then the opening question. The briefing frames the session; it never summarizes the sources.
  why: the user wants to know what they're walking into before thinking starts; context lowers the cost of starting
  not: opening cold on the tension in two or three lines (no frame for why it matters or what the payoff is); a recap of what the sources say (a reading digest, not a conversation)
  by: human
